## Design Context

### Users
Internal project owners, delivery team members, finance and approval staff, and administrators use the browser or iPad workbench to review project health, resolve work, and verify delivery progress.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, precise, operational. The interface should help people scan real project facts and act without obscuring status or permissions.

### Aesthetic Direction
The main workbench follows the Mist & Deep-Sea Console system: cool white and deep navy surfaces, cyan for information paths and focus, lime for actions and completion, and restrained semantic colors for risk. Use Chinese sans-serif typography, square geometry, fine borders, and both light and dark themes. Keep `/login`, `/spark`, and `/stream-monitor` within their documented visual boundaries.

### Design Principles
- Keep the work surface readable and data-led; decoration stays behind content.
- Preserve the same semantic hierarchy across light and dark themes.
- Use color with labels, shape, or position so state is never color-only.
- Prefer borders and spacing over nested cards, heavy shadows, and rounded containers.
- Keep motion purposeful, brief, and optional under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain keyboard access, mobile touch targets, and responsive layouts.
